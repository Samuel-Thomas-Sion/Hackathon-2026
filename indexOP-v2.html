      Why Not Just Paste Into a Generic Chatbot?

      Generic AI Chatbots
      • ✕Gives long, generic summaries without pinpointing the exact predatory sentence.
      • ✕No objective risk scoring—leaves you guessing whether a clause is normal or dangerous.
      • ✕Requires manual prompting every time and gives no ready-to-send pushback reply.
      ClauseGuard Engine
      • ✓Isolates verbatim predatory quotes and explains the exact financial or privacy trap in plain English.
      • ✓Computes a weighted 0–100 Safety Score alongside a 1-click copyable negotiation reply.
      • ✓Includes an AI follow-up assistant, 4 specialized modules, and direct 1930 / 1915 escalation links.
